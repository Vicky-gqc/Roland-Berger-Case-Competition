Prompt: The Commercial Journey of Humanoid Robots — Opportunities, Challenges, and Scenario Deployment
In recent years, humanoid robot technology has experienced rapid development due to breakthroughs in artificial intelligence, sensor technology, and materials science, and is moving from the laboratory into reality. Potential applications in scenarios such as industrial manufacturing, medical care, and household services are widely discussed. However, controversies have arisen: questions about the necessity of a humanoid form, the high R&D costs, insufficient technological maturity, and ethical and safety concerns have led some to consider humanoid robots a “false trend.” Against this background, how can we rationally assess the commercial value of this market? Which links in the chain truly hold deployable commercial opportunities?
Please analyze the overall feasibility of large-scale commercial deployment of humanoid robots at the current stage from two core dimensions: commercial value and commercial feasibility.
Specific analysis requirements:


Analysis of commercial feasibility —
• List two industries that you believe have the greatest potential for commercial deployment of humanoid robots (i.e., “relatively feasible compared with non-humanoid robots”), and provide a detailed justification of the core reasons for their feasibility (focus on both commercial value and commercial feasibility).
• List two industries that you believe are least likely to see short-term commercial deployment of humanoid robots (i.e., “relatively infeasible compared with non-humanoid robots”), and provide a detailed justification of the key reasons for their infeasibility (also consider both commercial value and commercial feasibility).


Market-entry feasibility analysis —
Assume your company is an automobile manufacturer. Based on your company’s resource endowments, analyze the advantages and disadvantages of entering the humanoid robot market. Beyond full-system manufacturing, which core components should you prioritize capturing and why?
